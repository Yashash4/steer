South Asians aged 45 and over in Chennai, Delhi and Karachi, BMI under 25 kg/m2: diabetes risk was 5.27 times higher in women (95% CI 3.64 to 7.54) and 2.99 times higher in men (95% CI 2.09 to 4.28) compared with US whites.
In South Asians aged 45 and over in Chennai, Delhi and Karachi, the authors suggest obesity and insulin resistance may not be the driving factors behind high diabetes incidence, and impaired insulin secretion may be more important.

Sources: Narayan KMV, Kondal D, Daya N et al. (2021) BMJ Open Diabetes Research & Care, doi:10.1136/bmjdrc-2020-001927
