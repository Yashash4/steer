Scale move kar raha hai, toh lean mass ki chinta ho rahi hai?
Obesity wale patients mein, jinhe obesity doses par GLP-1 receptor agonists diye gaye, absolute lean mass -1.74 kg badla (95% CI: -3.04 to -0.45); results studies ke beech mein varied a lot between studies (I² = 98%).
7 studies (821 patients) ke meta-analysis mein, obesity wale patients jinhe obesity doses par GLP-1 receptor agonists diye gaye, unka lean mass share of total weight ke roop mein 1.81% badha (95% CI: 1.1–2.52).
In studies mein muscle strength ya physical capacity test nahi hui, isliye in changes ka clinical matlab limited hai. Authors ke hisaab se treatment ke saath nutrition and exercise ko jodna essential hai.
