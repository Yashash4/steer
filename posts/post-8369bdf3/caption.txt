Worried about lean mass when the scale moves?
In patients with obesity treated with GLP-1 receptor agonists at obesity doses, absolute lean mass changed by -1.74 kg (95% CI: -3.04 to -0.45); results varied a lot between studies (I² = 98%).
In a meta-analysis of 7 studies (821 patients) of patients with obesity treated with GLP-1 receptor agonists at obesity doses, lean mass as a share of total weight rose 1.81% (95% CI: 1.1–2.52).
The studies did not test muscle strength or physical capacity, so what the changes mean clinically is limited. The authors call it essential to pair treatment with nutrition and exercise.
