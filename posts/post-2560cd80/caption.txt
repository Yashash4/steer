This is an average across all weight management medicines, not only GLP-1, and not a prediction for you: adults with overweight or obesity regained 0.4 kg monthly on average after stopping (95% confidence interval 0.3 to 0.5).
Among 28 randomised controlled trials, weight in treatment groups came back to the control groups' level 1.4 years after stopping.
