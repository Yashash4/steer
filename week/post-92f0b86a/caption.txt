On average, weight came back 0.4 kg a month (95% confidence interval 0.3 to 0.5) after stopping weight management medicines in adults with overweight or obesity. It is an average across all weight management medicines, not only GLP-1, and not a prediction for you.
Among 28 randomised controlled trials, weight came back to the comparison group's level 1.4 years after stopping. This is an average across all weight management medicines, not only GLP-1, and not a prediction for you.
