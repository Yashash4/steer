Paper to post, every claim checked

An open-source pipeline that checks each health claim against the paper it came from, and a quiz game built from the claims that passed.

  • The Real Number: a short quiz game (play it on a phone)
  • Review studio · Finished posts: read-only snapshot of every claim, its status and the review history
  • Sources: one table per paper (markdown and CSV)

Reels: see the Drive link, REEL_DRIVE_LINK.

Independent open-source project. Not official Steer code and not medical advice. No Steer clinician has reviewed anything here.
