Dinner is done, the chai is cold, and the question stays: what am I losing along with the weight?
In patients with obesity treated with GLP-1 receptor agonists at obesity doses, absolute lean mass changed by -1.74 kg (95% CI: -3.04 to -0.45; p < 0.00001); results varied a lot between studies (I² = 98%).
A meta-analysis of 7 studies (821 patients) found that lean mass as a share of total weight rose 1.81% (95% CI: 1.1 to 2.52; p < 0.00001; I² = 7%) in patients with obesity treated with GLP-1 receptor agonists at obesity doses.
The studies did not measure muscle strength or physical capacity, so clinical meaning is limited. The authors call it essential to pair treatment with nutrition and exercise.
