Thinking about GLP-1 medicines and wondering about side effects? Fair question.
In trials, higher doses of this class of medicines were linked to higher risks of nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation and decreased appetite. A doctor weighs the benefits against the side effects.
Only one trial looked at the risk of abdominal discomfort in patients receiving GLP-1 medicines.
Before you start, hear it from a doctor, not a reel.
